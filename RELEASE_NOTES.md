**Sendling 1.0.1**

A big feature update on top of 1.0.

**Sending**
- **Two drop wells** — **Send** (as-is) and **Compress** (zip everything into one archive first).
- **Finder integration** — right-click any file → Services → **Send with Sendling** or **Compress & Send with Sendling**, without opening the app.
- **Watched folder** — point Sendling at a folder; anything dropped in uploads automatically.

**Links**
- **Protected & expiring links** — set a per-account link password and auto-expiry (Nextcloud; Dropbox needs a paid plan).
- **Copy as Markdown / HTML** — grab `[name](url)` or an `<a>` tag for any file.
- **QR sharing** — drag the QR code out as a picture, or use the Share button.

**Sync & setup**
- **iCloud sync** — keep accounts and history in sync across your Macs via iCloud Drive (passwords stay per-Mac).
- **First-launch welcome** — offers to turn on sync, and adopts data another Mac already synced.
- **Test Connection** — verify a server's host, credentials, and directory before you rely on it.

**Quality of life**
- **Inline delete** — an `×` on every row (hold **⌥ Option** to skip the confirmation).
- **Retry failed uploads** and an **at-launch update check**.

Download `Sendling-v1.0.1.dmg`, open it, drag Sendling to Applications. Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Universal (Apple silicon + Intel), signed and notarized. Zero dependencies.
